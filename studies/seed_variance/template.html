Seed-variance study

How much do identical MoE runs separate?

Each curve is the sample standard deviation across seeds at the exact same optimizer step, plotted against cumulative training FLOPs. The default is training loss; the selector exposes every training and long-form diagnostic series recorded by both studies.

Historical optimizer boundary. Every run here predates commit 102a264672c8453700a02e321495a14c585e58ea. The earlier AdamW mask treated stacked rank-2 expert_up_b and expert_down_b bias tensors as weights and decayed them. The effect is expected to be minor, but a current checkout uses the corrected policy and cannot reproduce these trajectories bit-for-bit.

Estimator. Values are aligned by exact optimizer step and reduced with the sample standard deviation (n − 1). A tooltip reports the number of finite seeds contributing at that point. Hovering either chart places the crosshair at the same FLOP budget in both studies.

Resolution. The archived .riglog files remain full resolution. Curves longer than 1,440 samples are thinned only after the across-seed statistic is computed, using largest-triangle-three-buckets in FLOP coordinates. Endpoints are always retained.

Interpretation. The seed changes both initialization and shuffled training-data order. Raw training-loss and gradient variance therefore includes the loss composition of each seed's current batch; it is not a pure measure of parameter-state separation. The fixed final validation set is the cleaner endpoint comparison.
